## 🧠 THE FOXCONN LOGIC — WHAT PARAMETER WAS MISSING?

The original 3 documents had a **hidden filter bias**:

> **"If a company's primary revenue comes from manufacturing physical goods rather than software/services,
> exclude it — even if it has large software engineering teams doing exactly the right work."**

This is wrong. The correct filter should be:

> **"Does this company employ CS/IT/Embedded/AI engineers in production-grade engineering roles
> that match the candidate's skills — regardless of whether the company's primary business
> is software, hardware, manufacturing, defence, energy, or services?"**
